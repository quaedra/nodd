Share a model

Have a nodd-compatible model others could use? Submit a listing. Your model stays in your own Hugging Face repository.

  1. Publish your model. Include the nodd export, an open-source license, and a model card with evaluation results.
  2. Add a listing. Open models.json in the Files tab, choose Edit, and add your model using the example in the contribution guide.
  3. Submit for review. Choose “Open as a pull request.” After a maintainer merges it, your model appears here.

No organization membership needed to submit. Need a hand? Open a Community discussion with your model link.
